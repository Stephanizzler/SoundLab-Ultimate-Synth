SOUNDLAB Ultimate Synth

Sixteen classic synths and drum machines in one rack, played by a step sequencer you can patch to any knob. Everything runs on this device; nothing is uploaded.

Press Play or a key to start the sound.

1 · Rack

Eight slots. Load any machine into a slot; each is modelled on the architecture of a classic instrument. Open a slot to see its knobs.

2 · Sequencer

3 · Patch bay

4 · Play

5 · Render & save

Open a .soundlab project (to save into it or reopen a patch saved in it), a .json patch, or a .mid file (it goes onto the selected sequencer track).
